## Short-form video plan (TikTok / Shorts / Reels)

`marketing/jar-of-stars-trailer.mp4` is an auto-captured gameplay highlight
(golden piece → fever → bomb → storm). Post it as-is to start, but PHONE-RECORDED
clips with your thumb visible usually perform better. Three clip scripts:

1. **"Wait for it…"** — record a near-full jar, trigger SAVE ME at the last
   second, bomb clears the screen. Caption: "this game lets you UNDO death 💀📺"
2. **"Fever chain"** — chain x5+ into fever activation. Caption: "the screen
   literally catches fire when you're good 🔥" (sound ON — the pitch-rising
   merges carry it)
3. **"Storm rage"** — a storm ruining a perfect pile. Caption: "POV: the game
   has a weather system and it hates you ⛈"

Cadence: 2-3 clips/week, reply to every comment in hour one, remake the top
comment into the next clip. Always link `parthparmar06.itch.io/jar-of-stars`
in bio.
